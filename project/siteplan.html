Argentina Pizza Tour

Website Planning Document

1. Site Name & Branding

Proposed Name: Argentina Pizza Tour

The name is direct and effective, clearly stating my site's focus (Pizza) and location (Argentina)

2. Site Purpose

The site serves as a definitive cultural guide dedicated to the unique style, history, and pizzerias of Argentine pizza, primarily focused on Buenos Aires. It aims to educate international visitors (like, fugazza, "muzzarella al molde" and pizza a la parrilla), provide curated tour information and shop recommendations, and support local, traditional "pizzerias"(pizza's shops) by sharing their heritage with the world.

Scope: The homepage will provide an introduction to the history and distinctiveness of Argentine pizza. Other pages will offer a Tour Guide, specific Pizzeria Recommendations, and in-depth Culture/History articles.

3. Scenarios (Target Audience Questions)

These questions will drive the main content of the website and ensure it serves the target audience (tourists, foodies, and curious locals).

Scenario 1: The Hungry Tourist

"I'm near the Obelisco in Buenos Aires right now. Where can I find a traditional, classic Argentine-style *muzzarella* pizza that locals love, and what are the opening hours?"

Scenario 2: The Curious Foodie

"I've heard about 'Pizza a la Parrilla' (Grilled Pizza). What exactly is it, and are there any highly-rated shops that specialize in making it with a good *Provolone* topping?"

Scenario 3: The Planner

"I'm visiting Argentina for one week and want to focus on the oldest, most historic *pizzerias*. Can I find a pre-planned route or guided tour covering the essentials and their founding history?"

4. Color Schema

Primary Background

#680002 (Bordó)

Role: Main body background, conveying depth, passion, and traditional restaurant feel.

Primary Accent (Text/UI)

#C5A844 (Gold)

Role: Headings, primary links, and borders. Suggests quality and premium content.

Accent Hover/Shadow

#9D8006 (Dark Gold)

Role: Used for hover effects on buttons and interactive elements, providing depth and feedback.

Secondary Accent (Dark Teal)

#0097B2

Role: Separators, footers, and decorative lines. Provides a vibrant contrast.

Secondary Accent (Light Teal)

#41AEC8

Role: Future background color for specific pages (e.g., Tour Guide) or highlighted elements.

Text Color

#F3F4F6 (Light Cream)

Role: Main body and paragraph text for high legibility against the dark background.

Supplementary Colors (Logo/Detail):

These are reserved for small graphic details, like logo elements or icons, to provide rich complexity.

5. Typography

Headings (H1, H2, H3) - Sancreek, cursive

Role: Used for section titles and the main site name. The display font provides a bold, unique, and classic feel, honoring the long history of Argentine food traditions.

Body/UI Text - Inter, sans-serif

Role: Used for all paragraph text, navigation links, and button labels. The modern sans-serif font ensures maximum readability and a clean, user-friendly interface.

6. Wireframe (Home Page)

The wireframes illustrate the proposed layout for the Home Page, incorporating the decorative elements from the initial sketch (header, interactive blocks, and footer band).

Mobile View

Features a stacked, single-column design. Navigation is collapsed into a menu button. The interactive gallery stacks vertically.

Wide Desktop View

Features a multi-column layout. Navigation is inline. Main content is centered, and the interactive gallery is displayed in a responsive 3-column grid.
